STAGE SIZE (STAGE TO BACK ROW)
Calibrate in the room
  1. Put the device on the tripod at the back row.
  2. Tap Start listening.
  3. Have an adult say a line from the stage at a good performance volume, and tap the button while they speak.
Hearing now--
Find your stage voice
You need a helper to play the audience. Pick a quiet spot where nobody else is talking.
  1. Your helper stands far away, as far as your audience will be if you can. A gym, hallway or backyard works well.
  2. Hold this device about one arm's length from your mouth (2 feet).
  3. Tap Start listening.
  4. Say "Testing, one, two, three!" to your helper in a big, strong voice. Don't scream.
  5. Your helper gives a thumbs up only if they understood every word without straining. No thumbs up? Try again a little louder.
  6. Right after the thumbs up, tap Save my stage voice. It keeps your loudest moment from the last 10 seconds, so there's no rush.
Try it 2 or 3 times and save the one that felt right.

USE THIS GOAL FOR
Goal at that distance
Sound drops about 6 dB each time the distance doubles. Echoes in big rooms make real readings a little higher, so for large spaces a quick check in the actual room is still the most accurate.
Lower makes the light easier to turn on.
